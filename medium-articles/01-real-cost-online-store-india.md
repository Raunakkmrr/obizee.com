# The Real Cost of Running an Online Store in India [2026 Breakdown]

*Most ecommerce platforms show you a monthly price. Here's what Indian sellers actually end up paying — and how to find a platform that fits your budget.*

---

Starting an online store in India should not cost a fortune. But when Indian small business owners look at the actual cost of running their store, the numbers often tell a different story than the marketing page.

This is not about bashing any platform. Premium ecommerce tools are genuinely great — for the right business at the right scale. But if you are a small Indian seller doing 30–200 orders per month, you deserve to know the **real cost** before committing.

## The Advertised vs. Real Cost Gap

Most premium ecommerce platforms in India advertise plans starting at ₹2,000–5,000/month. That looks manageable. But the monthly subscription is just the starting point.

Here is what a typical Indian small business actually pays:

**Subscription plan:** ₹2,000–5,000/month
**Transaction fees (2% on 100 orders × ₹800):** ₹1,600
**Shipping app (third-party integration):** ₹500–1,000/month
**Email marketing app:** ₹500–1,500/month
**Reviews/social proof app:** ₹0–500/month
**Premium theme (amortized):** ₹400–800/month

**Realistic monthly total: ₹5,000–₹8,000** — for a small seller on an entry-level plan.

The app marketplace on many platforms is both their greatest strength and their biggest additional expense. Features that are built-in on some platforms — shipping, email, reviews — require paid add-ons on others. A seller using 3–4 essential apps can easily add ₹2,000–4,000/month to their bill.

## Where Premium Platforms Win

Being fair — premium platforms are the better choice in several scenarios:

- **International selling:** Multi-currency, international shipping, global payment gateways.
- **App ecosystem:** Thousands of apps for every conceivable need — loyalty programs, subscription boxes, advanced analytics.
- **Enterprise scaling:** If you are growing fast and need features like headless commerce or multi-currency checkout.
- **Advanced customization:** Custom themes, checkout experiences, API integrations.

## Where They Fall Short for Indian Sellers

- **No built-in shipping for India:** You typically need a separate app (like Shiprocket) — adding cost and complexity.
- **Pricing in USD mindset:** Many apps and themes are priced in dollars. A $20/month app costs ₹1,700+.
- **Overkill for small catalogs:** If you sell 10–50 products via Instagram and WhatsApp, you're paying for features you'll never touch.
- **Monthly commitment regardless of sales:** Even zero-sale months cost you the full subscription plus app fees.

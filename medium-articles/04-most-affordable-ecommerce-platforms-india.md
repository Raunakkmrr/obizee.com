# 10 Most Affordable Ecommerce Platforms for Indian Sellers [2026 Guide]

*We ranked 10 ecommerce platforms available in India from cheapest to most expensive — based on real costs, not marketing pages.*

---

Starting an online store in India should not require a massive upfront investment. But with so many platforms available — each with different pricing models, hidden fees, and confusing tiers — figuring out which one is actually the cheapest is harder than it should be.

We ranked **10 ecommerce platforms** from cheapest to most expensive, based on real costs: monthly subscription, per-order fees, and what you actually get included. All prices are as of April 2026, calculated for a typical small seller doing 50–100 orders per month at ₹800 average order value.

## The Rankings

### #1. oBizee — ₹0/month + 1% per order (max ₹10)

**Best for:** Small Indian sellers who want built-in shipping.

No subscription at all. Built-in Delhivery and DTDC shipping integration. 3-month free trial. The 1% fee is capped at ₹10 per order, so your costs stay predictable even as order values grow.

*Strength:* The only budget platform with native shipping integration included.
*Limitation:* Newer platform with a smaller ecosystem compared to established players.

### #2. Dukaan — ₹417/month (billed yearly at ₹4,999)

**Best for:** WhatsApp-focused sellers who want marketing tools.

Strong WhatsApp selling tools. Established brand in India with marketing features like abandoned cart recovery and discount codes.

*Strength:* Solid WhatsApp integration and growing plugin marketplace.
*Limitation:* Annual billing only — ₹4,999+ upfront before your first sale. No built-in shipping.

### #3. Bikayi — ₹333/month (billed yearly)

**Best for:** Basic WhatsApp-based stores on a budget.

Affordable entry point. WhatsApp catalog integration and basic store features.

*Strength:* Low per-month cost on annual plans.
*Limitation:* Limited design customization. Shipping handled separately.

### #4. DM2buy — Free to start (commission-based)

**Best for:** Instagram-only sellers who value community.

Built for Instagram sellers. Strong seller community with IRL meetups and workshops.

*Strength:* Excellent for Instagram DM-based selling. Active seller community.
*Limitation:* Instagram-only focus. Commission-based pricing (details may vary). No logistics integration.

### #5. Instamojo — ₹0 free plan / Premium from ₹999/month

**Best for:** Digital products, payment links, and services.

Good payment links feature. Works for both physical and digital products.

*Strength:* Versatile payment platform that handles digital downloads too.
*Limitation:* Per-order fees (2–5%) add up quickly. Store features are basic on the free plan.

### #6. WooCommerce — ₹300–800/month (hosting costs)

**Best for:** Technical users who want full customization.

Open source with unlimited customization potential. Full ownership of your store.

*Strength:* No platform fees. Complete control over design and functionality.
*Limitation:* Requires WordPress, hosting, PHP knowledge. Security and maintenance are on you.

### #7. Ecwid — ₹0 free plan / Paid from ₹1,200+/month

**Best for:** Adding a store to an existing website.

Can embed into any existing website. Decent free tier for up to 10 products.

*Strength:* Works alongside your existing site without rebuilding anything.
*Limitation:* Free plan is very limited. Paid plans get expensive for Indian sellers.

### #8. Shopify — ₹2,000+/month

**Best for:** Larger businesses selling internationally.

Best app ecosystem in the industry. Excellent for international selling and large catalogs.

*Strength:* Thousands of apps, global payment gateways, enterprise-grade scaling.
*Limitation:* Expensive for small Indian sellers. Many essentials require paid apps, pushing real costs to ₹5,000–8,000/month.

### #9. Wix — ₹1,300+/month

**Best for:** Design-first brands and portfolios.

Beautiful templates. Drag-and-drop builder is very beginner-friendly.

*Strength:* Stunning out-of-the-box designs without any coding knowledge.
*Limitation:* Not built for the Indian market. Limited INR-native payment and shipping integrations.

### #10. Squarespace — ₹1,400+/month + 3% on Basic

**Best for:** Portfolio-style stores and creative brands.

Stunning design quality. Great for showcasing creative work alongside products.

*Strength:* Best-in-class design templates for creative professionals.
*Limitation:* Most expensive option. Poor India-specific payment and shipping support.

## What Makes a Platform Truly "Cheap"?

Monthly subscription is only part of the story. The real cost includes:

- Per-order fees and payment gateway charges
- Shipping app subscriptions
- Marketing and email app costs
- Theme or plugin costs
- Setup fees or annual lock-in

A platform that looks free upfront can cost thousands once you add the essentials. Always calculate the **total cost of ownership** at your expected order volume.

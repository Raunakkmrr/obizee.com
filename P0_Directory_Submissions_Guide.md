Core features include: auto-generated merchant website, order dashboard (Pending → In Progress → Shipped → Delivered), inventory management, custom form builder with dynamic fields, Delhivery & DTDC logistics integration, fare calculator, multi-link support, employee & vendor management, and payment tracking.

Perfect for clothing sellers, jewellery businesses, food businesses, kirana stores, handicraft sellers, and anyone looking to sell online in India without technical knowledge.
